# Gentle Summer Bridge for Sensitive & Gifted Kids

**Shared with love** from our family's private homeschool journey (May 2026).

We are building a calm, interest-led summer bridge for our neurodivergent rising 1st grader who loves bunnies, colors, stories, and nature.

## Core Principles
- Short joyful sessions (15-25 min)
- Built-in movement & sensory breaks
- Daily emotional check-ins ("What color is your feeling?")
- Zero pressure, lots of celebration
- Follow the child's lead and energy

## Bunny Math Ideas (Easy to Copy)
**Addition:** "Help the bunny family count their carrots!" (sums ≤ 10)
**Subtraction:** "Some bunnies hopped away — how many are left?"

Use real objects first, then paper. Add bunny drawings and stickers for fun.

## Color Words + Bunny Coloring
Create a simple tracing page with color words (RED, BLUE, etc.) and a small bunny to color next to each word. Combines handwriting, color recognition, and fine motor in a playful way.

## Daily Gentle Rhythm Example
Morning: Color Monster check-in + short activity + movement break
Afternoon: Story/art/nature time
Evening: Read-aloud + heart hug

Total learning time under 1 hour. The rest is for play, rest, and connection.

## Sensory Safety Tips
- Quiet corner ready
- Noise-cancelling headphones available
- Visual timer for short bursts
- Validate every feeling
- End with breathing or hugs

These small things make a big difference for sensitive hearts.
