# ☀️ Savannah's Summer Bridge Plan – Gentle 1st Grade Prep

**A low-pressure, joyful 10-week plan for sensitive, deep-feeling kids**  
June through early August • 15–30 minutes most days • Built with mindfulness and emotional safety

## 🌿 Philosophy
This is **not** school. It’s summer magic that gently keeps curiosity alive, supports emotional regulation, and builds confidence for fall — all through nature, stories, art, and play.

## 🧘‍♀️ Mindfulness Moments (Use Daily)
- **Ocean Breath** – Breathe in like smelling the sea, out like blowing on a shell  
- **Heart Hug** – Cross arms, squeeze gently, say something kind to yourself  
- **5-4-3-2-1 Senses** – Name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, etc.  
- **Nature Stillness** – Sit quietly outside for 1–3 minutes  
- **Color Monster Check-In** – “What color are you feeling today?”

## 📅 10 Weekly Themes

### Week 1: Ocean & Beach Wonders
- Collect shells/rocks and sort by size/color  
- Read *The Rainbow Fish* or *Commotion in the Ocean*  
- Draw or paint your favorite sea creature  
- **Mindfulness**: Ocean Breath while holding a shell  
- Nature walk: count birds or waves

### Week 2: Sand & Dunes
- Build a mini dune in a tray with sand  
- Experiment: what sinks/floats in water?  
- Story: *The Sandcastle* or dune ecology book  
- **Mindfulness**: Nature Stillness imagining you’re on a tall dune  
- Sensory play: dry vs wet sand textures

### Week 3: Wetlands & Swamp Explorers
- Make a simple food chain with drawn animals  
- Read about frogs or cypress trees  
- Boardwalk nature hunt (quiet observation)  
- **Mindfulness**: 5-4-3-2-1 while near water  
- Draw your “calm swamp spot”

### Week 4: Flight & Invention
- Paper airplane contest with different designs  
- Watch short Wright Brothers video for kids  
- Build a cardboard glider  
- **Mindfulness**: Heart Hug before trying something new  
- Talk about trying hard things (like the Wright brothers)

### Week 5: Gardens & Growing Things
- Plant seeds or herbs in cups  
- Measure growth daily  
- Read *The Tiny Seed* by Eric Carle  
- **Mindfulness**: Water plants while doing Ocean Breath  
- Nature journal: draw what you see growing

### Week 6: History Heroes & First Settlements
- Simple Roanoke or Jamestown story  
- Make a “time capsule” box  
- Dress-up or puppet show of explorers  
- **Mindfulness**: Color Monster bravery check-in  
- Map activity: Virginia Beach to Outer Banks

### Week 7: Lighthouse Keepers & Stars
- Build a paper lighthouse  
- Night sky watching (or glow stars)  
- Read about lighthouse life  
- **Mindfulness**: Nature Stillness under the sky

### Week 8: Marine Life & Aquariums
- Virtual or in-person aquarium visit  
- Create an ocean diorama in a shoebox  
- Learn 5 sea creatures and their babies  
- **Mindfulness**: Ocean Breath with blue calm-down jar

### Week 9: Trails & Forest Friends
- Easy hike at First Landing or similar  
- Bug or leaf rubbing art  
- Animal tracking stories  
- **Mindfulness**: 5-4-3-2-1 on the trail

### Week 10: Celebration & Reflection
- Make a Summer Memory Book  
- Review favorite activities  
- “Win Jar” – read all the good things  
- **Mindfulness**: Big Heart Hug for all the growth  
- Plan one special field trip

## 💡 Tips for Sensitive Days
- Shorten activities or skip to mindfulness  
- Use noise-cancelling headphones outside  
- Offer choice between 2 activities  
- Always end with a calm connection moment

## Materials (Mostly Free)
Art supplies, nature finds, library books, cardboard, seeds, sensory bins
