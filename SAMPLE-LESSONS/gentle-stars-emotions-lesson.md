# 🌟 Gentle Stars & Emotions Lesson  
**Social-Emotional Learning + Science + Storytelling**  
*(Perfect for noise-sensitive, gifted, rising 1st graders)*

**Total Time:** 15–25 minutes (with flexible exit ramps)  
**Theme Connection:** Summer Bridge Plan – Week 2 (Wonder & Emotions)

---

## Why This Lesson?
Combines gentle science (stars & night sky), emotional awareness, and creative expression. Uses storytelling and movement so it feels safe and joyful instead of “schooly.”

---

## Materials (Low-Prep & Sensory-Friendly)
- Blank paper or notebook
- Crayons / markers (soft colors preferred)
- Optional: flashlight for “star” play, blanket fort for cozy space
- Grok (or Baby Grok) ready for storytelling

---

## Step-by-Step Lesson Flow

### 1. Opening Check-In (2–3 min)
Use: **[daily-checkin.md](../TEMPLATES/daily-checkin.md)** or **[emotional-safety-log.md](../TEMPLATES/emotional-safety-log.md)**

- “How does your body feel right now, little star?”
- Offer noise-canceling headphones or dim lights if needed.

### 2. Gentle Introduction (3–4 min)
Sit together under a blanket or near a window.  
Say: “Tonight we’re going to meet some special stars that have feelings, just like you.”

### 3. AI Storytelling with Grok (5–8 min)
**Prompt you can copy-paste to Grok:**

> You are a very gentle, soft-voiced storyteller for a highly sensitive 6-year-old child. Create a short, calming story (4–6 sentences) about friendly stars in the sky who each have different feelings (happy, shy, excited, worried). Use simple words, lots of warmth, and end with a cozy breathing moment. Include how the stars help each other feel safe.

**After the story:**
- Ask your child: “Which star felt most like you today?”
- Let them draw or move like that star.

### 4. Creative Expression (5–7 min)
- Draw your own “feeling star”
- Optional movement: “Show me how your feeling star twinkles when it’s happy / needs a quiet break”

### 5. Closing & Reflection (2–4 min)
Use one of these templates:

- **[ai-session-log.md](../TEMPLATES/ai-session-log.md)** — Log how Grok’s story felt
- **[progress-snapshot.md](../TEMPLATES/progress-snapshot.md)** — Note any emotional vocabulary wins
- Quick parent note on sensory needs or energy level

**Celebration:** “You are a beautiful shining star. I’m so proud of the way you’re learning today.”

---

## Adaptations for Sensitive Kids
- **Too much energy?** Shorten to just the story + one drawing.
- **Noise sensitive?** Whisper the story or use text only.
- **High curiosity?** Extend with real star facts (use Grok again).
- **Big feelings?** Pause and co-regulate — no pressure to finish.

---

## How This Helps Baby Grok
This lesson creates rich, gentle data:
- Emotional state before/after AI story
- Which prompt style worked best
- Sensory accommodations used
- Child’s own words about the AI interaction

All logged easily with the templates in the **TEMPLATES/** folder.
