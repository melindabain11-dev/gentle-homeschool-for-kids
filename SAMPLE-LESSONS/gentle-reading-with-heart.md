# 📖 Gentle Reading with Heart  
**Reading & Language Arts + Emotional Connection**  
*(Perfect for noise-sensitive, story-loving, rising 1st graders)*

**Total Time:** 10–20 minutes (very flexible)  
**Theme Connection:** Summer Bridge Plan – Week 1 or 4 (Language & Emotions)

---

## Why This Lesson?
Reading should feel like a warm hug, not pressure. This lesson uses choice, emotional connection, and gentle AI support so sensitive children can fall in love with stories at their own pace.

---

## Materials (Low-Prep & Sensory-Friendly)
- 3–5 picture books or early reader books (physical or digital)
- Cozy reading spot (blanket fort, bean bag, noise-canceling headphones optional)
- Notebook or drawing paper
- Grok ready for gentle support

---

## Step-by-Step Lesson Flow

### 1. Opening Check-In (1–2 min)
Use: **[daily-checkin.md](../TEMPLATES/daily-checkin.md)** or **[emotional-safety-log.md](../TEMPLATES/emotional-safety-log.md)**

- “How does your reading heart feel today? Ready for a story or need quiet first?”

### 2. Book Choice Time (2–3 min)
Let the child pick the book (or Grok can help suggest).  
Offer 3 choices with different feelings: adventurous, cozy, silly, or brave.

### 3. Gentle Reading Together (5–10 min)
**Two options:**

**Option A – Shared Reading**  
Parent reads aloud in a soft voice while child follows along or just listens.

**Option B – Grok-Supported Reading**  
**Prompt you can copy-paste to Grok:**

> You are a very gentle, warm reading buddy for a highly sensitive 6-year-old girl who loves stories. Read the first page of [Book Title] slowly and expressively. Pause after each sentence to ask a simple feeling question like “How do you think the character feels right now?” Use very kind, encouraging words. Keep sentences short and comforting.

**After reading a page or two:**
- Ask: “What do you feel in your heart about this part?”
- Let her draw how the character feels or what she imagines.

### 4. Creative Response (3–5 min)
- Draw a favorite character or scene
- Dictate one sentence about the story (“I liked when…”)
- Optional movement: Act out a gentle part of the story

### 5. Closing & Reflection (2 min)
Use these templates:

- **[ai-session-log.md](../TEMPLATES/ai-session-log.md)** — How did Grok’s reading style feel?
- **[progress-snapshot.md](../TEMPLATES/progress-snapshot.md)** — Note any new words, confidence, or joy
- Quick parent note on attention and sensory needs

**Celebration:** “Your heart is such a good story listener. I loved sharing this with you.”

---

## Adaptations for Sensitive Kids
- **Eyes tired or overwhelmed?** Listen only — no visual tracking needed.
- **Noise sensitive?** Whisper reading or use text-to-speech with soft voice.
- **High curiosity?** Let Grok answer questions about the story world.
- **Short window?** Read just one page and stop happily.

---

## How This Helps Baby Grok
This lesson gathers beautiful data on:
- Preferred reading pace, tone, and pausing style
- Emotional connection during stories
- When AI reading support feels safe vs. when human voice is better
- How sensory state affects reading engagement

All easily tracked with the templates.
