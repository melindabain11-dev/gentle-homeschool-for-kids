# Sample Lesson: Identifying & Naming Big Feelings – The Color Monster

**Best for:** Highly sensitive, empathetic, or "feeler" children (ages 5–8)
**Duration:** 15 minutes
**Skill Focus:** Recognizing and naming emotions

## Why This Works Beautifully for Sensitive Kids
This lesson uses story and color to give children language for big feelings in a playful, non-threatening way. It is quiet, choice-based, and ends with connection.

## Sensory & Emotional Adaptations
- Quiet story time in a calm corner
- Noise-cancelling headphones option
- Plenty of time for emotional processing
- Validate every feeling (no right or wrong)
- Art response as gentle regulation tool
- Built-in choices throughout

## Lesson Steps
1. Cozy up in a quiet spot together (2 min)
2. Read *The Color Monster* (or watch a calm, low-volume version) (5 min)
3. Ask gently: "Which color feels like you right now?" (3 min)
4. Soft discussion: "What makes that color feel that way for you?" (3 min)
5. Optional: Draw your own Color Monster (2 min)
6. End with a hug + breathing exercise (1 min)

## Materials
- The Color Monster book or calm read-aloud
- Paper + crayons
- Cozy blanket

## Tips for Success
- Let the child lead the pace
- Celebrate whatever feeling they name
- Repeat often — it becomes a beautiful daily check-in tool
